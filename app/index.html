Writeback functions

writeback_forecast(id, kg, user, expected_version, note)Sets a planner override on one forecast cell. Rejects actual (locked) periods, negative values and stale versions, so two planners editing the same cell can't silently overwrite each other.
writeback_bulk_pct(ids[], pct, user, note)Applies a % uplift or reduction to many cells in one transaction.
add_plan_comment(id, category, body, user)Attaches business context such as Tender pending or Customer confirmed to a cell.
resolve_plan_comment(comment_id, user)Closes a comment once the information is no longer pending.
